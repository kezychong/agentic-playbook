# Build Track

**Agentic Playbook — by Kezy**

One order for building an always-on assistant with an eight-door second brain. Several packs refer to "Stage N": this page says what each stage is and which package does it. Go at your own pace; a stage can take an hour or a week. Every install, schedule, connection and payment along the way is still a separate approval.

| Stage | What you build | Package |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Prepare: one brain folder in your laptop home folder, request your AI chat exports, a coding agent on the laptop | (no package) |
| 2 | One computer that never sleeps | [Server Setup](../08-setup-guides-tools-connections/server-setup/README.md) |
| 3 | Reach it from anywhere: private network, phone, desktop app | [Connect My Machines](../08-setup-guides-tools-connections/connect-my-machines/README.md), [Telegram Topics](../08-setup-guides-tools-connections/telegram-topics/README.md), [desktop app guide](../08-setup-guides-tools-connections/server-setup/guides/desktop-app-window.md) |
| 4 | Mirror the folder to the box, build the eight doors and the rulebook | [Second Brain V3](second-brain-v3/README.md): Brain Folder Setup, Brain Installer |
| 5 | Capture whole sources; park your chat export; the about-you interview | [Capture Bot](capture-bot/README.md); Second Brain V3: Drop Your Chats, Digital Twin Builder |
| 6 | The Brain Map, then the link, file and count bots | Second Brain V3: Digital Twin Builder (Part E); [Link, Organise and Signal](link-organise-signal/README.md): Link, Organise and Signal Bots |
| 7 | Rest or catch up | — |
| 8 | List every tool, pick its route; add keys safely | [Connect Your Tools](../08-setup-guides-tools-connections/connect-your-tools/README.md): Connect Your Tools; [Add a Key](../08-setup-guides-tools-connections/add-a-key/README.md) |
| 9 | WhatsApp and every tool into the nightly capture | Connect Your Tools: Connect to Capture (or [WhatsApp Capture](whatsapp-capture/README.md)) |
| 10 | The bots run every night; a search engine for the brain | Link, Organise and Signal: Dreaming and Recall |
| 11 | Prove the brain remembers: a score out of 100 | [Score Your Brain](score-your-brain/README.md) |
| 12 | Judge your Signals; find testable hunches | Link, Organise and Signal: Insights Pass |
| 13 | Light day: trace one note from capture to its final door | — |
| 15 | Your assistant interviews you about your work | [Chief of Staff Interview](../03-jarvis-setup/chief-of-staff-interview/README.md) |
| 16 | The rulebook and boot files from that interview | [Chief of Staff Interview](../03-jarvis-setup/chief-of-staff-interview/README.md), [Basic Onboarding](../03-jarvis-setup/basic-onboarding/README.md) |
| 17 | Answers as pages, not walls of words; share a page as a link | [Eli5](../01-agentic-starter-kit/eli5/README.md), [HTML Output](../08-setup-guides-tools-connections/html-output/README.md), [here.now Publishing](../08-setup-guides-tools-connections/herenow/README.md) |
| 18 | One page every morning | [Morning Brief](../03-jarvis-setup/morning-brief/README.md) |
| 19 | Turn what works into skills | [Learn Skill](../04-ai-team-builder/learn-skill/README.md), [Skill Levelup](../04-ai-team-builder/skill-levelup/README.md) |
| 20+ | Light days and re-checks: read your briefs, re-run Score Your Brain weekly, grow it over time | Score Your Brain |

## Prefer the simpler vault?

[Second Brain Setup](second-brain-setup/README.md) builds a five-folder vault run from your laptop, with [Digital Twin](digital-twin/README.md) for the about-you profile. Pick one layout per brain, not both.
