# Capture Router: Install & Use

Collection: Agentic Playbook — by Kezy. Use the included canonical skill folder.

Send your agent a link, quote, document, screenshot, or thought.
It checks your saved notes, files new material, and gives you the exact path.
Your second brain is your collection of notes, like a searchable notebook.

Works in Claude Code and in Codex.

## What you need first
- The Claude app or the Codex app.
- A notebook folder your agent can read and write.
- The downloaded `ap-capture-router.zip`.

## Install
Open your agent and say:

> Install the skill in my downloaded ap-capture-router.zip so I can use it in every project.

Your agent handles installation and any needed requirements.
It tells you if your app cannot read a source.

## Check it worked
Tell your agent:

> Use ap-capture-router to save this thought: Compare flour delivery options.

On the first run, it asks where notes belong and whether you want a contents link.
It asks one question at a time and saves your answers.
A successful check ends with a saved note's path or an existing note's path.

## How to use it
1. Send a link, attachment, quote, or thought to your agent.
2. Say: "Capture this." Add why it matters if you know.
3. Your agent reads what it can and checks your notebook for matching material.
4. NEW means no match was found. PARTIAL means an existing note covers some of it.
5. COVERED means you already have it. Your agent returns that note instead of creating a duplicate.
6. Read the final line for the exact path and any reading limits.

## What it will NOT do
- It will not summarize a page it could not read.
- It will not invent your reason for saving something.
- It will not move your existing notes or publish your captures.
- It will not call an incomplete search a successful duplicate check.
- It labels unverified attribution "assumed, not checked".
