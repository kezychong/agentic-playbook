# Phone chat prompts

**Agentic Playbook — by Kezy**

For thinking sessions in a phone chat app (ChatGPT, Claude or similar) that cannot run this skill. Paste one prompt at the end of the chat, then send the result to your capture channel (for example your [Telegram room](../../../../../08-setup-guides-tools-connections/telegram-topics/README.md)) so it lands in your Inbox.

## /keep — the ideas that changed my mind

```text
List the ideas from this conversation that actually shifted my thinking. For each one, write a
standalone note: what the idea is, why it matters to me, and enough context that I would understand
it in six months without rereading this chat. Write in my voice, not as a summary of what we discussed.
Mark anything you are unsure I agreed with as "not confirmed".
```

## /article — the whole thinking, written up

```text
Turn this whole conversation (my reasoning, my questions, where I pushed back, and your answers)
into one clear article for my notes. Keep my conclusions as mine, keep open questions open,
and do not add facts that were not in the conversation.
```

When the note arrives, Capture Session's approval step still applies: nothing becomes a durable note until you confirm it.
