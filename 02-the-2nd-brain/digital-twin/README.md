# Digital Twin

**Agentic Playbook — by Kezy**

Digital Twin captures **who you are**: it reads your exported AI chats into small notes, interviews you, and builds a `digital-twin/` folder plus draft wiki articles in your voice. Cartographer then maps **what you do**: it spots recurring work domains in those notes and gives each a home. Built for the five-folder [Second Brain Setup](../second-brain-setup/README.md) vault and run in Claude Code; resumable at every phase. For the eight-door brain, use [Digital Twin Builder](../second-brain-v3/skills/ap-digital-twin-builder/README.md) instead. A local source draft, not an installed or runtime-tested system.

## Included skills
- [Digital Twin](skills/ap-digital-twin/README.md): Turn your AI chat history and a 30-question interview into a living profile of how you think, with draft wiki articles you approve.
- [Cartographer](skills/ap-cartographer/README.md): Find the recurring kinds of work in your notes and map each into its own Area, one decision at a time.

## Setup
1. Read each included skill README and its prerequisites.
2. Keep every skill folder and all its support files together.
3. Review the documented host setup before copying anything into an installation.
4. Use fictional inputs first. Loading a skill does not approve installation, external actions or spending.
