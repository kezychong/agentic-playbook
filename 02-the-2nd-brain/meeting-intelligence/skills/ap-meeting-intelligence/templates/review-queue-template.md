# Review queue template

Agentic Playbook — by Kezy · blank template, not a live record.

One row per item flagged during a meeting-intelligence pass. The human answers the Decision column
with `yes`, `no`, `change: <value>`, or `later`.

```markdown
| # | Type | Quote / line | Question | Best guess | Decision (yes/no/change/later) |
|---|------|--------------|----------|------------|----------------------------------|
| 1 | speaker | "..." | Who said this line? | ... | |
| 2 | term | "..." | Is "..." the correct spelling/name? | ... | |
| 3 | contradiction | "..." vs "..." | Which statement is correct? | unresolved | |
```

Type values:
- `speaker` — unclear attribution of a line to a speaker label.
- `term` — unclear spelling, name, or term not already in the glossary.
- `contradiction` — two statements in scope that cannot both be true.
