# Relationship Wiki

**Agentic Playbook — by Kezy**

A private relationship wiki inside your second brain: one note per person, what you know and where you learned it, open promises both ways, and a short brief before you meet or message them. People are added only with your yes. An original starter method for this collection. A local source draft, not an installed or runtime-tested system.

## Included skills
- [Relationship Wiki](skills/ap-relationship-wiki/README.md): Keep one sourced note per person you deal with, link them to your life areas, and brief you before a meeting or message.

## Setup
1. Read each included skill README and its prerequisites.
2. Keep every skill folder and all its support files together.
3. Review the documented host setup before copying anything into an installation.
4. Use fictional inputs first. Loading a skill does not approve installation, external actions or spending.
