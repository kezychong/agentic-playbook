# Shelf Menu: standard shelves for a business folder

A shelf is a subfolder inside a business folder in `03. Areas/`, organized around what something is FOR, not which app or export it came from. During setup, propose 3 to 7 shelves per business from this menu, based on what the owner said the business does. Custom shelf names are welcome; this menu is a starting point, not a limit.

## The menu

- **Offer & Economics**: what the business sells, pricing, packages, commission or margin structure.
- **Sales & Follow-Up**: scripts, pipelines, and steps for turning leads or members into paying clients.
- **Leads & Follow-Up**: the lead list and follow-up notes, for businesses where working the list IS the work.
- **Marketing & Content**: content research, scripts, campaign notes, and ideas for social or viral content.
- **Content & Proof**: ad creative, testimonials, results screenshots, anything used to prove the offer works.
- **Community**: running a member community (Telegram, WhatsApp, Skool, Discord): posting rhythm, moderation, engagement.
- **Partner Recruitment**: how referral partners, affiliates, or resellers get found, qualified, and signed.
- **Operations & Team**: who does what, and how the business runs day to day outside sales and marketing.
- **SOPs & Process**: step-by-step guides for repeatable tasks, one document per task. (SOP is short for Standard Operating Procedure, a written step-by-step.)
- **Documents & Templates**: proposals, application templates, and other boilerplate the business reuses often.
- **Sales Playbook**: a complete sales system (message templates, qualification method, stages) that deserves its own shelf.
- **Claims & Compliance**: what's safe to say publicly (income claims, guarantees) and any regulatory considerations. Sensitive: check with the owner before anything here goes public.

## How to choose

- Listen to the one-sentence business description. A community-driven business needs Community and Content & Proof. A service business drowning in leads needs Leads & Follow-Up and SOPs & Process. A business with a sales team needs Sales Playbook.
- Fewer, fuller shelves beat many empty ones. Start with what the owner's actual documents will land on; add shelves later when something has no home.
- Every business gets a README listing its shelves (use `README-template.md` in this folder).
