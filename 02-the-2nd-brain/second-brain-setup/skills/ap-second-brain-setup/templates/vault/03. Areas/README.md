# Areas

Areas are the ongoing pillars of the owner's world, the parts that never "finish": each of their businesses, and their personal life. Unlike projects, there's no end date here.

There is one folder per business (created during setup, named what the owner actually calls each business), plus `Personal/`.

Inside each business, knowledge is organized into "shelves": subfolders built around what something is FOR, not which app or export it came from. For example, everything about a business's sales scripts lands on its "Sales Playbook" shelf, whether it started life as a PDF, a ChatGPT conversation, or a voice note. This way, the owner finds things by what they're trying to do, not by hunting through export folders.

Open each business folder's README for its own shelf list and what belongs on each one.
