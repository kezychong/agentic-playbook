# Linking Rules

Wiki links look `[[like this]]`. Typing two square brackets, the note title, and two closing square brackets connects one note to another. Obsidian shows these as clickable links and draws a map of how notes connect to each other.

## The rules

- Every distilled note (an atomic idea pulled out of something bigger) links back to the source note it came from. Use a `Source:` line, see `_System/templates/atomic-note.md` for the shape.
- Every note about a specific business links to that business's shelf `README` in `03. Areas/`. That's how you find everything related to one business from a single starting point.
- Linking to a note that doesn't exist yet is fine. Obsidian shows it differently, often in a lighter color, and that's a good thing. It marks a gap or a future note waiting to be written.
- Prefer a few links that actually matter over many weak ones. A note with three links the owner would actually click is more useful than one with fifteen links nobody will ever follow.
