# Fictional example — Company A proposal desk

Agentic Playbook — by Kezy

Input: Company A has requested three proposal drafts over the last month. The owner supplies
only `approved-offer.md` and three redacted call transcripts. No agent currently owns proposals.
The target output is two proposal drafts per week, with each term linked to its call evidence.
No CRM access, messaging or spending is authorized. All names and files here are fictional.

Trigger: “Use ap-hire-an-agent to interview me and stage a proposal assistant. Do not install it.”

Expected result: a one-job description (“prepare evidence-backed proposal drafts”), a preview of
allowed read paths, draft-only rules, staged identity/manual files and a first draft assignment.
The agent explains that activation has not happened. It records probation as three approved drafts.
If a transcript lacks price, the proposal says “price awaiting confirmation,” not an invented fee.

Review brief: every term must cite evidence; no price may be invented; output must stay local.
A fresh judge should mark a draft with an invented price NEEDS_REPAIR or FAIL according to the
brief's severity, identify the exact line and prescribe removal or source confirmation.
This is an expected result, not a recorded runtime test.
