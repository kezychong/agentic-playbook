# Hire Your Next Hermes Agent

**Your Hermes agent interviews you like an HR manager, then builds your next Hermes agent for you: a new AI employee with one job.**

## Who this is for

- You are not a technical person. That is fine.
- You work inside the Claude app or the Codex app. Most members run their agent through the Codex app.
- You already finished the Chief of Staff onboarding. Your agent already exists, and it already knows your name, your business, and your goals.
- Your agent does all the setup. You just answer questions.

## What you need first

The Claude app or the Codex app, with your Chief of Staff agent already set up. That's it.

## Install

Download the zip, open your agent, and say:

```
Install the skill in the selected complete skill folder
```

Your agent unpacks it and installs it into its own skills, itself. You never touch a file.

## How to use it

Say this, exactly:

**"I want to hire a new Hermes agent."**

Your agent takes it from there.

## What happens next

The interview happens once per hire. It takes about 10 minutes of questions, asked a couple at a time, never a wall of them at once.

Once that hire is built, you talk to them whenever you want. Your agent will hand you one exact phone line, one sentence, for reaching your new hire from now on. You never need this download again for that hire.

## What you get

- A new named AI employee with ONE job. Not a jack of all trades. One job, done well.
- It only ever hands you drafts. Nothing goes out, nothing gets spent, until you say yes.
- It only sees a short, named list of your files, like a filing cabinet pass to a few specific drawers, not the whole office.
- It starts on probation. Like any new hire, it earns more trust after a few pieces of good work.

## What this is NOT

- It never replaces your Chief of Staff. Your Chief of Staff still runs the show. This just adds one more desk to the office.
- It will not push a hire on you. If the work does not come back often enough, your agent will recommend something smaller instead. The final call is always yours: if you still want the hire, it builds the hire.
- It will not let a new hire spend money or contact anyone outside your business without your yes first.

## Not sure this is for you yet?

Paste this instead: **"Teach me how hiring a second agent works, then walk me through whether I need one."**
