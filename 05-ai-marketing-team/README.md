# AI Marketing Team

Agentic Playbook — **by Kezy**

A practical collection for turning supplied evidence and your own ideas into drafts, creative briefs, videos and presentations. These nine skills retain their supplied methods and supporting source tools. Kezy maintains the repackage; this does not claim independent authorship or grant new downstream licences.

Start with [setup and permissions](SETUP.md), then follow [the workflow](WORKFLOW.md). Try [Company A’s fictional workshop campaign](examples/company-a-workshop.md) before using real material. Its [expected result](examples/expected-result.md) is an authored illustration, not a runtime test.

| Skill | Use it for |
|---|---|
| [Headline Analyzer](headline-analyzer/skills/ap-headline-analyzer/README.md) | Score headlines and interpret split-test evidence. |
| [Edit Pipeline](edit-pipeline/skills/ap-edit-pipeline/README.md) | Plan and edit talking-head videos with review gates. |
| [Retargeting Ads Core Funnel](retargeting-ads-core-funnel/skills/ap-retargeting-ads-core-funnel/README.md) | Design warm-audience ads and funnel exclusions. |
| [Ad Analysis and New Creatives Brief](ad-analysis-and-new-creatives-brief/skills/ap-ad-analysis-and-new-creatives-brief/README.md) | Inspect ad evidence and brief the next creative batch. |
| [Newsletter from Voice](newsletter-from-voice/skills/ap-newsletter-from-voice/README.md) | Turn voice notes into evidence-led newsletter drafts. |
| [Yap](yap/skills/ap-yap/README.md) | Develop truthful short talking-camera video maps. |
| [Content OS](content-os/skills/ap-content-os/README.md) | Organize content from capture through review and learning. |
| [Instagram Carousel](instagram-carousel/skills/ap-instagram-carousel/README.md) | Storyboard and export readable Instagram carousel slides. |
| [Presentation Builder](presentation-builder/skills/ap-presentation-builder/README.md) | Build clear browser-based decks from supplied evidence. |

Use one skill on its own or combine them. Content OS can hand a recording to Edit Pipeline; both retain a manual route. No external account is needed to try the text examples. Rendering, transcription and the dashboard have additional prerequisites.

## Blank working files

- [Campaign intake](templates/campaign-intake.md): audience, evidence, offer and scope.
- [Evidence and claim ledger](templates/claim-ledger.md): what each claim can support.

The detailed source templates remain beside their skills: newsletter settings, Yap profiles/maps/tracker, Content OS workspace templates, creative brief and ad memory tracker, presentation starter and carousel design rules. Copy blanks into a chosen working folder; keep installed source assets separate from real answers.

## Limits

Everything is a local draft package. No host, source script, renderer, integration, scheduler or account connection was exercised. Marketing scores and audience heuristics are planning aids, not guarantees. Obtain human review of truth, rights, accessibility and claims before use. Nothing sends or publishes merely by loading a skill. Paid tool calls, installations and account changes require approval.
