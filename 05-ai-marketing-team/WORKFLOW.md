# From evidence to a reviewed campaign

Agentic Playbook — by Kezy

## 1. Capture the truth

Complete [intake](templates/campaign-intake.md). Content OS separates what the owner believes from how a piece is packaged: the owner’s angle stays blank until supplied. Qualify the idea, then use its truth interview and evidence packet. Yap is the shorter route for an honest talking-camera clip: choose a niche, interest or life lane, interview the owner, apply the kill list and taste gates, and create a talking map.

## 2. Match the reader and the container

Use Headline Analyzer to establish awareness, sophistication, desire and the page’s one job before scoring its eleven dimensions. Keep the source’s gating criteria and test variants across different angles. A rewrite may sharpen a true promise; it may not invent proof, a deadline or a result.

For an email, Newsletter from Voice verifies reader opt-in and recognition, preserves the speaker’s experience, selects a framework, checks claims, and produces subject choices and a draft. A recipient signing up does not prove they used a download. Keep draft staging separate from sending; this skill never sends or schedules.

## 3. Make the appropriate asset

For a carousel, storyboard the 5–10 slide arc, select a design template, build the 420×525 preview and export at 1080×1350 only after setup and visual review. For a presentation, build the story outline first, use a supplied moodboard and starter, and inspect each 16:9 slide. Rendering and browser inspection must happen at use-time; this package has not done them.

For recorded video, Edit Pipeline transcribes, proposes marker-based cuts and waits for the owner’s review. Watch the full master before requesting platform exports or captions. Keep original footage, proposed cut list and reviewed master distinct. Store large media outside synced text folders.

## 4. Brief paid creative from observed ads

Ad Analysis uses authorized account exports or approved read access, inspects images and sampled video frames, records coverage gaps, reads the memory tracker and classifies awareness levels. Produce the decision card and brief before generating anything. The source’s batch size is configurable; agree the generator cost before execution. Retargeting Core Funnel separately maps warm audience layers and exclusions. A campaign plan is not permission to create audiences, spend money or launch ads.

## 5. Approve the exact version

Fill [review and handoff](templates/review-handoff.md). Match every number and quote to [the evidence ledger](templates/claim-ledger.md). Verify that the delivered files exist and that visual checks actually happened. Stop at local drafts unless the user separately authorizes the next action. Content OS stages the plan; the owner publishes.

## 6. Learn from measured results

Log actual views, opt-ins, spend and period in [the experiment ledger](templates/experiment-ledger.csv). Headline relative lift and sample-size heuristics are directional signals, not statistical significance tests or proof the headline caused the outcome. Content OS compares like content jobs, records missing attribution honestly and uses observed patterns to propose the next test. Record hypothetical or remembered figures separately from measured evidence.
