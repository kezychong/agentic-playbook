# Brand Strategist

Agentic Playbook — **by Kezy**

Develop evidence-led brand strategy and activation plans.

This is an original, generic public workflow. It does not include named proprietary brand methods or external training material.

## Prerequisites

A defined brand or offer, the business objective, intended audience, known constraints, and permission to use the supplied research.

## Try it

> For fictional Company A, compare three positioning options for a new bookkeeping service. Mark assumptions and use only the evidence in this brief.

## Expected output

An evidence map, diagnosis, three materially different strategy options, a decision record, an activation plan and a measurement plan.

## Install and use

Read [the complete method](SKILL.md) and [local setup](INSTALL.md). Copy the whole folder. Run a harmless fictional sample before allowing real or confidential data.

## Limits

Runtime status: **untested**. Strategy quality depends on the evidence supplied and research actually performed. Drafts require human fact-checking and decision. No automatic installation, account access, publication, spending or changes to live brand assets.
