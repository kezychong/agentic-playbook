# Content OS

**Version:** 1.2.1 · Updated 2026-08-24

## What it does

Installs a full content system into your agent. It interviews you once about your business, your audience, how you talk, and the real stories behind what you sell. From then on it takes an idea you had in the car, checks it against what is already working in your niche, scores it, and hands you a filming map in your own words with the proof lines built in.

After you film, it cuts the raw recording into a clean master, turns that one recording into posts for every other channel, lays out exactly what to post where, and writes down what happened when you did. One board shows you the whole picture on a single page.

You stay the one who decides what is true and what gets published.

## What is new in this version

Version 1.1 stopped at the drafts. This version carries the work all the way through.

1. **Your recording gets edited for you.** You film in one go and say your marker word every time you fumble. Your agent finds every one of those moments, removes the bad attempt in front of it, shows you the list before it cuts anything, and hands you a clean master to watch. Then three sizes for the different places you post. Captions if you want them. The cutting is done by the free Edit Pipeline pack, and without it your agent walks you through the same cut by hand.
2. **A record of what you posted, with the numbers beside it.** Every piece you publish gets written down once, and the numbers get added each time you read them, with the date they were true. Nothing is ever overwritten. Six months from now you can still see exactly what worked.
3. **Real arithmetic on your own results.** Your normal, worked out from your own last twenty comparable pieces. How far above it each piece landed. Which shapes keep winning for you. When there is not enough data yet, it says so instead of guessing.
4. **A posting plan and a checklist.** Which file, which words, where, in what order. Five minutes instead of an hour of second-guessing. You still press post. Nothing here ever posts for you.
5. **The Cockpit.** One page in your browser, on your own computer, showing everything: what to do today, your whole pipeline as a board, what is working in your niche, the news your audience cares about, your calendar, and your library. Say "open my cockpit" and it opens. Close the window and it is gone.

Already set up on an earlier version? Nothing is lost and you never redo the interview. Your agent offers the upgrade once, adds what is missing in about a minute, and leaves everything you already wrote exactly as it is.

## What you need first

The Claude app or the Codex app. That is it. Everything in this pack works with nothing installed.

The cutting of your videos is done by a free companion pack called **Edit Pipeline**, available in the included `ap-edit-pipeline/` companion folder. It is optional. If you have it, your agent hands your recording straight to it. If you do not, your agent walks you through the same cut by hand in whatever editor you already have, and you still finish the piece that day.

## Check it worked

Say `/ap-content-os` to your agent. It should notice you have not been set up yet and offer to start the setup interview. That interview takes about twenty minutes and it asks one question at a time, so you can stop and pick it up later.

## How to use it

1. Say yes to the setup interview the first time. It asks about what you sell, who buys, where you post, how you talk, a few real stories from your work, and a handful of accounts you want to learn from. Paste in a few of your own past posts when it asks, that is what makes the writing sound like you.
2. From then on, drop ideas at it whenever they land: "content idea: the client who fired me and came back nine months later."
3. Once a week, open two or three posts from your watchlist that clearly did better than that account normally does, and paste them in with "research drop". It pulls out why they worked and files it.
4. You do not need to ask for the overnight radar. Setup switches it on for you. From that first night it keeps an eye on your notes and on any new call transcripts, and files the strong moments you wrote or said as idea cards while you sleep. Angles stay blank, they are yours. Most nights it finds nothing and stays quiet. When it does find something, a short summary is waiting for you in the morning. Anything you saved from someone else is left alone. One sentence pauses it, points it at a different folder, or removes it.
5. When you want to make something, say "which idea should I make" and it scores your inbox against seven things that decide whether a piece lands.
6. Say "script this idea" and it interviews you, then hands you a filming map: two exact opening lines, the middle as bullets in your own words, one exact closing line.
7. Film it in one go, saying your marker word every time you fumble. Then say "edit my video" and point at the recording. You get a clean master to watch, and the sizes you need.
8. Say "multiply this" to get the email, the community post, the carousel outline, and the thread.
9. Say "stage my post" for the plan and the checklist. You press post.
10. The moment it is live, say "it's posted" with the link. A day later and three days later, read the numbers off your screen when it asks. That is what makes the weekly review worth having.
11. Say "open my cockpit" any time you want to see all of it on one page.

Example: "Capture this idea: three clients this month asked me the same question in the first ten minutes, and it is the wrong question."

## What it will NOT do

- It will not decide what you believe. It captures and researches everything around your angle and leaves the angle itself blank for you.
- It will not post anything anywhere. Every draft comes back to you first, and publishing stays your hand on the button. There is no connected account and no stored password for anywhere you publish, and there is no setting that changes that.
- It will not write your whole script. Your opening and closing lines are exact, the middle stays bullets on purpose, so what comes out of your mouth is yours and not a recital.
- It will not decide your edit is fine. It shows you what it removed before it cuts, and you watch the master before anything else happens to it.
- It will not invent a number. If a number is missing it says so, because a made-up number quietly poisons every decision after it.
