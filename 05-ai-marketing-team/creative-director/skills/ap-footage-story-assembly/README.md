# Footage Story Assembly

Agentic Playbook — **by Kezy**

Assemble permitted footage into evidence-led stories with consent and claim controls.

## Prerequisites

A complete permitted source set, playback context, target format, speaker identities, consent scope, claim ledger, disclosure duties and reviewers.

## Try it

> Build a cut list for three fictional speaker clips. Keep the fictional result types distinct, hold one clip with pending consent and stop before rendering.

## Expected output

A source inventory, verified transcript status, consent and claim ledger, story spine, data-based cut list, local draft plan, verification record and hold list.

## Limits

Runtime status: untested source draft. No transcription engine, editor, renderer, storage account or publishing path was exercised. This skill does not post, send, spend or delete originals.
