# Edit Pipeline

**Version:** 1.0 · Updated 2026-08-24

## What it does

Edits your talking-to-camera videos for you, on your own computer, for free.

You film in one go. Every time you fumble a line you say a marker word out loud, take
a breath, and say the line again properly. You never stop the camera and you never
start again from the top.

Then you hand the recording to your agent and say "edit my video". It reads back every
word you said and the exact moment you said it, finds every marker, and removes the
fumbled attempt in front of each one. Before it cuts anything it shows you the list:
how many it found, what is going, and what each removed piece said. You look at the
list and say yes.

You get back one clean master to watch. Once you have watched it and you are happy,
you get the three sizes the different places want: tall for Reels, Shorts and TikTok,
square for feed posts, and wide for YouTube. Captions burned into the picture if you
ask for them.

## What you keep

One job. You watch the master once, all the way through, and you decide it is good
enough to go out. That is the deal. The machine takes the hours, you keep the
judgement.

## What you need first

The Claude app or the Codex app, and a video of you talking. That is it.

Two free tools get set up on the day of your first edit, by your agent. It tells you
what they are, says how long it takes, and asks before anything happens. Both of them
run on your own computer. Nothing is uploaded anywhere and nothing costs anything.

If your computer cannot install them, nothing is lost. Your agent walks you through
the same cut by hand in whatever editor you already have.

## Local installation

Use the manual folder-copy procedure above. No installation was performed during packaging.

## Check it worked

Say `/ap-edit-pipeline` to your agent. It should come back asking which recording
you want edited, and what word you said when you fumbled.

## How to use it

1. Film in one go. Say your marker word every time you fumble. "Cut cut" works. So
   does "snip snip". Pick one and always use the same one.
2. Say "edit my video" and point at the recording.
3. The first time, say yes to setting up the two free tools. This happens once.
4. Read the cut list your agent shows you. If anything on it is not what you remember
   doing, say so before it cuts.
5. Watch the master, all the way through.
6. Say yes and you get the three sizes.
7. Want captions on it? Say "add captions". Then watch it once more, because captions
   land on the wrong word sometimes.

Example: "Edit my video. It's the one I just saved to my desktop, and I said cut cut
about four times."

## Where your videos live

In their own folder on your computer, one folder per recording, outside anything that
syncs to the cloud. Your agent creates it and tells you where it is once.

This is a rule, not a preference. One ten-minute recording is bigger than every note a
content system will ever write for you, put together. A few of those in a synced
folder fill up the plan you pay for and slow your machine down while it uploads them.

## What it will NOT do

- It will not cut anything before showing you what it is about to remove.
- It will not export anything before you have watched the master.
- It will not take out your silences unless you ask. Automatic silence cutting is how
  edits get that choppy, machine-gun feel, so it is off by default.
- It will not put a watermark, a logo, or an intro bumper on your video. Ever. A
  rival app's watermark burned into a video is one of the cheapest ways to get shown
  to fewer people.
- It will not decide your edit is fine. You watch it. You call it.
- It will not post anything anywhere. It makes files. You publish.

## Works on its own, or with Content OS

This works on any video you point it at, with nothing else set up.

If you also run **Content OS**, the full content system that takes you from the idea
to the posting plan, this becomes its edit step. Its board hands the recording
straight over, reads your marker word from the rules you already gave it, and files
the record of the edit back next to that piece. You do not have to do anything to
connect them. Content OS is optional and nothing here needs it.
