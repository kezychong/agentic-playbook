## Test interpretation

Control: 25 ÷ 500 = **5%**. Variation: 36 ÷ 600 = **6%**. Absolute difference: **1 percentage point**. Relative lift: (6% − 5%) ÷ 5% = **20%**. The control has only 25 opt-ins; treat this as directional. No significance test was performed, and the scorecard cannot establish causation. Keep spend and CPL unknown.

The variation is more specific and names the free artifact. Awareness is provisionally solution-aware because the fictional search intent seeks a planning aid; sophistication remains an assumption to investigate. Apply the full eleven-dimension rubric before reporting a total score. Test a concrete artifact-led variant against a problem-led variant using the same true promise. Do not claim “saves hours” or “books more workshops.”
