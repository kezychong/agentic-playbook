# Headline Analyzer: Install & Use

A skill that does two jobs on your opt-in or landing page:

1. **Score & rewrite.** Paste your headline, answer 3 quick questions, get a score out of 100 plus 3 to 5 stronger rewrites you can test. Built on a century of split-tested direct-response method: awareness + sophistication diagnosis, the eight primal drives, the opening-angle playbook, and the specificity discipline.
2. **Analyze your split tests.** Paste your test numbers (page views, opt-ins, cost per lead, control vs variation) and it names the winner, explains WHY using the same method, keeps a running ledger of every test you have run, reads the trend across all of them, and tells you exactly what to test next.

---

## What you need first

- The Claude app or the Codex app.
- A project you open the app in (your second brain vault works, or any project folder).
- The downloaded `headline-analyzer-v2.zip` (your agent unzips it for you).

## Local installation

Use the manual folder-copy procedure above. No installation was performed during packaging.

## Check it worked

Open the Claude app in your project and tell it:
```
/ap-headline-analyzer
```
If the skill is installed, it will start the review. You can also just say "analyze this headline for me" or "which of my headlines won?" and it will trigger on its own.

## How to use it: scoring a headline

1. Paste your headline and ask your agent to analyze it.
2. Answer the 3 short questions it asks: who the page is for, what they are opting in to get, and where the traffic comes from. (This is the whole game. A headline can only be judged against the person reading it.)
3. Read the diagnosis: your market awareness level, your market sophistication stage, and a score out of 100.
4. Use the rewrites. It hands you 3 to 5 variants, each on a different angle, each labelled with why it fits your market. Pick 2 to 3 and split-test them.

## How to use it: analyzing a test

1. Paste your numbers: both headlines, page views and opt-ins for each, where the traffic comes from, and your cost per lead if you are running ads.
2. First time on a page, it will also ask for any past headlines you have tested and roughly what happened. Even rough memory counts. It logs everything into a test ledger it keeps for you.
3. Read the verdict: who won, by how much, whether the sample is solid, and WHY the winner won.
4. Get your next test: 2 to 3 new challenger headlines, each labelled with the hypothesis it tests.

Every test you feed it makes the next analysis smarter. The ledger becomes a private record of what YOUR market responds to.

## What it will NOT do

- It will not invent claims about your product. It rewrites what is true and sharpens it. If a rewrite over-promises, tell it and it will pull back.
- It will not call a winner from noise. If the gap between your two headlines is too small or the sample too thin, it says "keep running" instead of guessing.
- It is not a magic button. It tells you which 2 to 3 headlines to test. The market picks the winner, not the AI.
