# The Formula Library (swipe + adapt)

> These are guideposts, not laws - no formula works twice unchanged. Use them to generate variants fast, then let the diagnosis (awareness + sophistication) decide which one fits. Fill the `[BRACKETS]`.

**The four building blocks behind most opt-in headlines:**
- **RESULT** - the outcome they want, stated as a verb ("book 10 sales calls", "lose 20 lbs")
- **TIMEFRAME** - how fast ("in 30 days", "this week")
- **PAIN** - the thing they want to avoid, as an "-ing" action ("without cold DMs")
- **ROADBLOCK** - the "even if..." objection that stops them ("even if no one knows your name")

---

## Core opt-in / lead-capture formulas

**A. The How-To mega-structure** (the most reliable lead-magnet frame)
`How to [RESULT] in [TIMEFRAME] without [PAIN]... even if [ROADBLOCK]`
→ *How to book 10 sales calls a week without cold DMs, even if no one knows your name yet.*
Two modifiers is the sweet spot. Three is the max before it gets unreadable.

**B. The lead-magnet reveal** (workhorse for capturing email)
`[LEAD MAGNET TYPE] reveals how to [BIG RESULT] without [BIG PAIN]`
→ *Free checklist reveals how to book 10 sales calls a week without cold DMs.*
"Reveals" promises hidden info, the strongest opt-in trigger. The magnet must solve ONE specific, immediate problem, not the whole transformation.

**C. New [format] reveals** (opt-in hook story)
`New [Video / Report / Blueprint] reveals [SPECIFIC RESULT or SECRET]`
→ *New video reveals why some agency owners almost always profit from paid ads.*

**D. Webinar / masterclass registration** (sell the event, not the product)
`Attention [NICHE] who want to [BIG PAYOFF]: how to [DREAM 1] (and [DREAM 2]) without [FEAR 1] (or [FEAR 2]) in [TIMEFRAME]`
→ *Attention HDB owners who want to upgrade to private property: how to move up without risking your savings or timing the market, in one weekend.*
No product, no price. Stack their desires against their fears.

## Formulas by awareness level

**E. Solution-Aware result lead** (L3, fresh market S1-2)
`[Specific measurable result] in [specific timeframe]`
→ *Lose 20 lbs in 30 days.*

**F. Problem-Aware agitate + name** (L4)
`[Name the painful problem]? [Solution] stops [the specific torment].`
→ *Tired of lying awake at 3am worrying about money? This 1-page plan ends it.*

**G. Mechanism-led** (Stage 3-4, the most important upgrade for saturated markets)
`How to [RESULT] with the [NAMED MECHANISM]` / `Discover the [NAMED MECHANISM] that [PROMISE] without [PAIN]`
→ *Discover the Safe Entry Framework that sells your home for top dollar without paying agent commission.*
USP test for the mechanism: can a competitor say it? does the audience care? can you prove it?

**H. Identity / "that's me"** (Stage 5, or Unaware L5)
`For the [SPECIFIC IDENTITY] who wants [DESIRED STATE] back` / `To [AUDIENCE] who want to [RESULT], but can't get started`
→ *For the busy dad who wants his energy back.*

---

## Curiosity, proof, and pattern-interrupt formulas

**I. Enemy / insider secret** (curiosity + in-group identity)
`What [NAMED ENEMY] don't want you to know about [TOPIC]` / `The [TOPIC] solution that beats [ENEMY] at their own game`
→ *What real estate agents don't want you to know about selling your own home.*
The enemy must be specific and already resented. "The system" is too vague.

**J. Question / "who else wants"** (L4-5, cold)
`Who else wants [DESIRABLE OUTCOME] without [REMOVED PAIN]?` / `Are you tired of [PAIN]?`
→ *Who else wants a fully-booked calendar without cold outreach?*

**K. Numbered / listicle** (content opt-ins, social; use ODD numbers)
`[3 / 5 / 7] ways to [RESULT] and avoid [PAIN]` / `[N] mistakes that cost [AUDIENCE] [SPECIFIC LOSS]`
→ *7 mistakes costing agency owners $10k a month in wasted ad spend.*

**L. Story / status-flip classic** (L5 cold; a century-tested shape)
`They laughed when I said "I'm a [AUDIENCE]"... but when I started to [RESULT]!`
→ *They laughed when I said I was a copywriter, until I started landing $5k clients.*

**M. Social-proof classic** (belief through numbers of others)
`Thousands now [RESULT] who never thought they could` / `[Unlikely person] turns $[SMALL] into $[LARGE] in [TIMEFRAME]`
→ *Single mum turns $1,500 into $1M in 3 years.*

**N. Warning / loss-aversion** (cold, pattern-interrupt)
`Warning: if you want [RESULT] but you're worried about [PAIN], read this first`
→ *Warning: don't run another ad until you've set up this 10-minute standup.*
Loss framing pulls roughly twice as hard as the matching gain framing.

**O. Mistakes / "do you make these"** (L4 problem-aware curiosity)
`Do you make these mistakes with [TOPIC]?`
→ *Do you make these mistakes with your sales funnel?*

## The modifier layer (bolt onto any formula above)

- **Prefixes:** Finally! / New! / At Last! / Free! / Discover!
- **Suffixes:** ...Guaranteed! / ...Today! / ...Fast!
- **Superchargers:** money ("...and make your first $1,000"), timeline ("...in the next 7 days"), cost-removal ("...without spending a dollar on ads")
- **Pre-head flag:** put `Attention: [WHO]` above the main line to screen the right reader in and the wrong reader out before they hit the headline.

---

## Browse by psychological mechanism

When you know the emotion you want to pull, start here (each maps to formulas above):

- **Curiosity / information gap** → I, J, K, C ("reveals", "secret", "what X don't want you to know")
- **Fear / loss aversion** → N, O ("warning", "the mistake costing you...")
- **Desire / greed / aspiration** → A, B, E ("how to [result] without [pain]")
- **Specificity / numbers** → E, K, M (real figures, odd numbers, timeframes)
- **Social proof / authority** → M, "top [X] expert reveals...", "who else wants...?"
- **Identity / "that's me"** → H, L, "for the [identity] who..."
- **Paradox / pattern interrupt** → "How a [paradoxical person] [got result]", "Why traditional [topic] advice is wrong"
- **Newness / urgency** → C, modifier prefixes ("New!", "Announcing")
- **Mechanism (the saturated-market fix)** → G ("the [Named Method] that...")

---

## The testing mindset (bake this into the recommendation)

- Expect to test 10 to 50 headlines before a clear winner. The best-vs-worst spread can be 5 to 10x the result. It is a numbers game, not a one-shot guess.
- Write 15 to 20 using different mechanisms above, sort by mechanism, pick 3 to 5 from DIFFERENT categories, then A/B test.
- Kill losers fast. Scale winners hard. Never get attached to a headline you liked.
- Dual-readership test: if a scanner reads only the headline and sub-headline, they should still get the full promise.
