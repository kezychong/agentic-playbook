## Preserved use guidance


## What it does

Turns one topic, article, transcript, or raw idea into a full Instagram carousel: a swipeable preview you can check on your laptop, plus 7 (or more) images sized exactly 1080x1350 for posting. Pick from 7 named design templates, or give it your own brand color. The text is rendered for real, not AI image generation, so words never come out garbled.

## What you need

The Claude app or the Codex app. That's it. Your agent sets up its own tools the first time you use the skill (about two minutes, once).

## Local installation

Use the manual folder-copy procedure above. No installation was performed during packaging.

## Check it worked

Ask your agent: "Do you have the ap-instagram-carousel skill?"

## How to use it

1. Give it a topic, article, transcript, or idea.
2. Say something like:
   - "Make me an Instagram carousel about [topic] using the Bold template."
   - "Turn this article into a carousel, use my brand color #1E3A5F."
   - "Make me a carousel about my morning routine, Warm template."
3. It builds a storyboard, generates the preview, and shows it to you before exporting.
4. Once you approve the preview, it exports the final images at 1080x1350, ready to post.

The first carousel takes a little longer while your agent sets up its design tools. It will tell you when it is doing that.

## What it will NOT do

- It will not use AI image generation for the slide text. Every word is rendered for real so nothing gets garbled.
- It will not skip the preview step. You see the carousel before final images are exported.
- It will not post anything for you. It hands you finished files, you post them yourself.
