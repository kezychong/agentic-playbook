## Preserved use guidance

# Newsletter from voice: Install & Use

Distribution: use the included complete skill folder. No external download is required.

Turn rough spoken thoughts into a newsletter or post using one of four writing recipes.
Your agent preserves your meaning, checks claims, and saves a draft in your voice.
When you ask, it can also place approved copy in your email tool as a draft.
It never sends or schedules.

Intended for compatible skill hosts; discovery and runtime compatibility are untested.

## What you need first
- Your agent app and the downloaded skill zip.
- Rough thoughts and two examples of your writing.
- Your usual email account, only if you want email staging.
Your agent may handle approved installation after explaining prerequisites and limits.

## Local installation

Use the manual folder-copy procedure above. No installation was performed during packaging.

## Check it worked
Say:

> Use ap-newsletter-from-voice to set up my newsletter settings.

It starts with your newsletter name and asks one question at a time.
It saves your answers and a short guide to your voice.

## How to use it
1. Dictate your rough thoughts into your agent.
2. Say, “Turn this into my newsletter using my saved writing recipe.”
3. Answer any questions about unclear words or missing evidence.
4. Review the draft, its checked claims, and five subject choices.
5. Choose your subject and approve the copy.
6. Say, “Stage this approved version in my email tool as a draft.”
7. Review the saved email draft using the link your agent provides.
For ideas first, say, “Scan my week for five newsletter ideas.”
For a post, say, “Turn these thoughts into a short post.”
A local draft and an email draft are separate outcomes.
If a check is unavailable, your agent says “assumed, not checked” and identifies the gap.

## What it will NOT do
- Send, schedule, publish, or send test emails.
- Invent experiences, results, reader history, or your voice.
- Include another person's real name or original material in public copy.
- Claim email staging succeeded without checking the saved draft.
