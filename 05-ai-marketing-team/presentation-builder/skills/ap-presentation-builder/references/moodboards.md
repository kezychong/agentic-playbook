# Moodboards

Use one board for the full deck. Do not mix palettes or font systems between slides.

## 1. Editorial Ink — default

Best for: teaching, strategy, leadership, premium workshops.

- Backgrounds: ink `#0B0C0F`, paper `#F5F6F9`, white `#FFFFFF`
- Primary accent: cobalt `#2850E8`
- Payoff accent: coral `#E85D4F`, once or twice per deck
- Main text: ink `#0B0C0F`
- Muted text: ash `#4A4F5A`
- Display and body: Hanken Grotesk
- Labels: JetBrains Mono
- Feel: strong headlines, quiet space, one connected visual

## 2. Warm Studio

Best for: coaching, culture, storytelling, community, creative work.

- Backgrounds: espresso `#1B1714`, cream `#FBF4E8`, white `#FFFDFC`
- Primary accent: ochre `#C77A2A`
- Payoff accent: clay `#C9573D`
- Main text: espresso `#1B1714`
- Muted text: walnut `#6E6258`
- Display: Fraunces
- Body: DM Sans
- Labels: JetBrains Mono
- Feel: editorial warmth, rounded corners, generous margins

## 3. Signal Blue

Best for: technology, systems, product, data, modern operations.

- Backgrounds: midnight `#07111F`, ice `#F3F7FF`, white `#FFFFFF`
- Primary accent: signal blue `#176BFF`
- Payoff accent: cyan `#25C9E8`
- Main text: midnight `#07111F`
- Muted text: slate `#52627A`
- Display and body: Space Grotesk
- Labels: JetBrains Mono
- Feel: crisp grids, thin lines, bold data, precise diagrams

## 4. Quiet Mono

Best for: finance, consulting, research, serious proposals, understated brands.

- Backgrounds: carbon `#111111`, stone `#F2F0EB`, white `#FFFFFF`
- Primary accent: graphite `#333333`
- Payoff accent: chartreuse `#B8D84A`, once per deck
- Main text: carbon `#111111`
- Muted text: gray `#66645F`
- Display and body: IBM Plex Sans
- Labels: IBM Plex Mono
- Feel: restrained, high contrast, precise rules, no decoration

## Matching a user's vibe

- "Premium, bold, clear" -> Editorial Ink
- "Warm, human, thoughtful" -> Warm Studio
- "Modern, tech, energetic" -> Signal Blue
- "Minimal, serious, elegant" -> Quiet Mono

If a user provides a brand color, replace the primary accent and derive a lighter wash from it. Keep the background, typography, spacing, and payoff-accent discipline of the chosen board.
