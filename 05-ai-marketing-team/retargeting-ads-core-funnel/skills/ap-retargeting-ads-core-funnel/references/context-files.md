# Context Files — audience.md and offer.md

Two small files that describe your customer and your offer, saved once in your project folder so you never have to re-explain them. Every ad the skill generates pulls from these plus the layer's awareness level. Build them once; reuse them forever.

## Where they live

Save both in the project folder you're working in for this business. If you don't have a project folder, make one (e.g. a folder named after the business) and keep them there. When you ask for ad copy later, just say "for `audience.md`, offer is `offer.md`" and the skill has everything it needs.

## If they don't exist — build them with the user

Ask the questions below, then write the two files. Keep them short and specific. Don't fabricate — if the user doesn't know an answer, ask them or leave a clear placeholder.

### audience.md — intake questions

- Who is the one person this offer is for? (One representative customer — role, situation, stage.)
- What is the core problem they have? What keeps them stuck?
- How do THEY describe that problem? (Their words, not marketing words.)
- What have they already tried that didn't work?
- What do they secretly want — the real outcome under the surface goal?
- Where do they hang out and consume content?

### offer.md — intake questions

- What exactly are you selling? (Name it.)
- What's the big promise / transformation?
- What's included? (The pieces, format, delivery.)
- What's the price and payment structure?
- Why should they act now rather than later?
- What's the single most common reason people hesitate?

## Templates

### `audience.md`

```markdown
# Audience (ICP)

## Who they are
[One representative customer — role, situation, stage of their journey.]

## Core problem
[The main problem this offer solves. What keeps them stuck.]

## How they describe it (their words)
[Direct language they'd actually use — pains, complaints, phrases.]

## What they've tried that failed
[Old solutions that didn't work.]

## What they really want
[The deeper outcome beneath the surface goal.]

## Where they are
[Platforms, communities, content they consume.]
```

### `offer.md`

```markdown
# Offer

## What it is
[Name and one-line description.]

## The promise
[The transformation / result.]

## What's included
[The components, format, delivery.]

## Price
[Price and payment structure.]

## Why now
[The real reason to act now.]

## Main hesitation
[The most common reason people don't buy — so ads can pre-handle it.]
```

## Keep them current

If the offer or audience changes, update these files. Every ad generated afterward inherits the change automatically — that's the point of keeping context in files instead of re-typing it each time.
