# Yap: Install and Use

## What it does

Your agent helps you find an idea and turn it into a yap you can film on your phone.
It writes your opening and close, with short bullets for the middle. You speak in your own words.
After recording, it marks what to trim. It also tracks which videos bring new followers.

## Who this is for

- You are a business owner or realtor who wants to talk on camera.
- You use the Claude app or the Codex app.
- You do not need technical skills. Your agent does all setup and file work.

## What you need first

- The Claude app or the Codex app.
- Your phone for recording.
- An idea, a news link, or a willingness to answer questions about your work, interests, and life.

## Local installation

Use the manual folder-copy procedure above. No installation was performed during packaging.

## Check it worked

Tell your agent:

```
/ap-yap
```

On the first run, it should ask questions to build your Yap Profile: your work, interests, life, and voice.
On later runs, it should ask for today's lane or suggest it from your saved weekly rhythm.
A lane means the kind of topic for today: your work, your interests, or your life.

## How to use it

Say, in your own words:

```
What should I yap about today?
```

```
Turn this into a yap script: <idea>
```

```
Here is my recording transcript, trim it.
```

Your agent helps you choose an idea, asks what really happened, and writes your yap map.
A yap map gives you exact edges and a bulleted middle. Film naturally, then ask your agent to mark cuts.
