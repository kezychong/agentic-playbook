# Find today's idea

## Weekly cadence

Post once a day using this starting rhythm. Adjust it during weekly review using the tracker.

| Day | Lane |
|---|---|
| Monday | Niche |
| Tuesday | Interest |
| Wednesday | Niche |
| Thursday | Interest |
| Friday | Niche |
| Saturday | Life |
| Sunday | Life |

## The three idea lanes

- Niche: work mistakes, questions customers ask, a costly mistake, or something learned at work this week.
- Interest: news the member has a real view on. Include, once in a while, a mass-market topic that has nothing to do with your niche, to bring new people to your page.
- Life: a glimpse of daily life, a journey update, or an honest feeling the member wants to share.

Use the confirmed profile. Propose five ideas for the chosen lane after applying the kill list.
Let the member choose. An idea must not claim an unconfirmed experience happened to them.

## Kill or reshape these ideas

| Reject or reshape | What to do |
|---|---|
| Advice without a fight | Find the real tension behind it through questions |
| Funny or trend-hopping at the start | Return to the member's thinking until they have followers |
| Needs a result the member does not have | Use a confirmed experience or choose another idea |
| Makes the member impressive but leaves the viewer out | Find the viewer's real situation and consequence |
| Competing fights in the same video | Keep a single fight and save the other idea |
| Corporate-brand voice | Return to the member's own words and judgment |

Do not repair an idea by inventing a result, feeling, or viewer history.

## Optional market scan

The member can say: "Help me find 30 to 50 creators in my market."
Ask whether they already follow creators whose videos they study.
Help them search Instagram Explore using words from their market and look at top-performing videos.
Have them look through the videos themselves first, to understand who they compete with.
The agent keeps the list in their profile. The member never has to maintain a file.
Use account links for the private list. Do not borrow a creator's speaking rhythm.
The agent can help more later. This scan never blocks making today's yap.
