# The yap method

## Rough talking

The content that works right now is not high production. It is you talking.
Film rough, then trim it so it stays under two minutes.

## More than your niche

Your niche is your area of work. You can also talk about news, interests, and life.
Your views beyond work help people get to know you. Share what you think about an announcement and why.
The supplied method emphasizes: "If you're always property, property, property, you are boring."
Fictional prompt example: share your view on a big national announcement, using a clearly hypothetical public-policy proposal rather than an unverified real announcement.
Do you think it will work? Are you a parent? "Just take any news and yap on it."

## A daily rhythm

Post once a day. Monday, Wednesday, Friday cover your niche. Tuesday and Thursday cover interests.
Saturday and Sunday give a glimpse into your life. Keep a system even when the recording feels casual.

## The law of 100

The first 100 videos are practice. Like gym reps, they help you improve by doing the work.
They also show you what people want. Going viral is not the goal of those first 100 videos.

## A niche and consistency

A friend of mine who sells landed property returned to that work after selling his company.
He chose a clear niche. With about 2,000 followers, a talking video reached 159,000 views.
He spoke about a costly property mistake: green screen with one picture behind him, just talking.
Developers and business owners reached out. People sent him this: "You look like you've been in property for twenty years."
You can attract a specific audience through a specific topic and consistency, even with a small following.

## A wider doorway

Sometimes talk about a topic many people care about, even when it sits outside your work.
Try a topic every parent is talking about, like a baby bonus announcement.
If one such video hits a million views, even 1% is 10,000 people who then find your page and discover your niche.

## Show how you think

Yapping lets people hear your judgment. Funny or trend videos can get attention without earning many follows.
In the teaching example, a funny trend video drew 500,000 views and under 100 followers.
A fictional calibration example: a video receives 30,000 views and 300 followers. This is not a verified result or a performance promise.
Not all content is the same. Views are not the goal, followers are.
Save funny and trend videos until you have followers. Start by showing how you think and what you know.

## Use both places

Post the same video on Instagram and TikTok. The property example gained more reach after being reposted on Instagram.

## Lead with yourself

We are in a personal brand era. Gone are the days of corporate branding.
People buy from someone they relate to. Generally men buy from men and women from women, so a couple can run two accounts on the same topic.

## Learn your market

Use Instagram Explore and search words from your market. Look at videos that perform well and follow their creators.
Build a list of 30 to 50 creators. Look yourself first so you understand who else speaks to your audience.
Your agent can help later. This scan is optional during setup and never delays today's recording.

## Share the journey

Share what you are trying and how it feels. Give people a reason to follow your journey.
An opener example: "I'm looking for a new job and I'm trying to use AI to help me find it. Follow me on my journey."
Use that only if it is true for you. You do not need to lead with awards.
Share the honest feeling and the vulnerable thought people at your stage actually have. That is what people relate to.
A creator I know began sharing while unsure about her next step. She went viral because she was real, with almost no editing.
She later built a business teaching others to yap.

## Let a picture help

Use a still picture or a green-screen image behind you when it makes the topic easier to understand.
A simple picture of a framework can make its steps clearer. Otherwise, your face is enough.

## Ads can come later

The supplied method emphasizes: "Many of my videos can be used as ads."
A strong yap may become an ad later. A coach we know reused a viral talking video as a successful ad.
Flag videos that earn follows as candidates. A candidate is a suggestion for review, not an instruction to spend.

## Taste before handover

Judge and rewrite every map before showing it. Read [the taste gates](yap-map-and-gates.md).
Check for a real fight, a viewer who sees themselves, proof, and the member's own voice.
If a repair needs a missing fact or feeling, ask for it. Never invent it.
