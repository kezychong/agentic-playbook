### Fictional worked example
A consultant helps small shops improve customer follow-up.
The consultant chooses proposal and action plan, with proposal as the default.
Proposals go to clients. Action plans go to the consultant's team.
Outputs go in Calls. The agent sets up transcription when a recording arrives.
The voice sample says: "Here is the next step we discussed. Please confirm the scope before we begin."
The proposal template includes the customer's problem, scope, stated price, next step, evidence, and open terms.
Its quality bar requires evidence for every term and visible labels for missing prices.
The action template includes actions, owners, dates, proof of completion, dependencies, status, and evidence.
Its quality bar requires missing owners and dates to remain awaiting confirmation.
These examples never fill your live settings automatically.
