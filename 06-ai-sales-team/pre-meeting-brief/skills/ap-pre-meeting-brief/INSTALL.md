# Pre-meeting Brief: Install and Use

Included locally: use this complete skill folder; no external download is required.

Get a short brief before a meeting, using messages and notes your agent can actually read.
It shows past discussions, promises, open questions, and three things to raise, with source links.
Missing records are labeled clearly.

Works in Claude Code and in Codex.

## What you need first
- The Claude app or the Codex app.
- The downloaded the included complete skill folder.
- Messages or meeting notes your agent may read.

Your agent handles installation and any missing requirements.
If an account needs sign-in, your agent guides that step.

## Install
Say this to your agent:

“Install the downloaded the included complete skill folder so I can use it in every project.”

## Check it worked
Say: “Use ap-pre-meeting-brief to help me prepare for a meeting.”
The agent asks one setup question at a time and saves your answers.
It confirms the saved settings and asks who you are meeting.
Installation alone does not prove it can read your accounts.

## How to use it
1. Say: “I'm meeting Sam in 20 minutes. Prepare my meeting brief.”
2. Give the company name if your agent finds more than one matching person.
3. Your agent reads only the sources you allowed.
4. It saves your brief and gives you its link.
5. On the move, you receive the brief directly in chat.
6. Say “Give me a phone page too” when you want a separate page.

The brief links every factual line to a message or note.
The complete evidence keeps the last three messages from each checked chat app.
A phone link needs a working sharing connection. Otherwise, your agent gives you the brief in chat.

## What it will NOT do
- Send messages or book meetings.
- Read chats you did not allow.
- Invent your history or pretend unread sources were checked.
- Set up a daily routine or publish your private notes automatically.
