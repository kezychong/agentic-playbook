# Progressive offer stack

Use one approved structured source for item names, benefit lines, values, images and totals. Preserve exact strings and calculate running totals in code.

When the owner requests progressive stacking, use four distinct screens for each item: item and tangible image, outcome benefit, individual value, cumulative restack. Keep every prior item visible unless approved grouping is requested. Highlight the newest row without making earlier items unreadable.

Choose truthful product imagery. Software boxes are for installable products. Use a workbook, planner, live-session or document form for those deliverables. Assigned values are not verified retail prices. Exclude separate fast-action bonuses from base totals unless the approved source includes them.

Check every slide for overflow, text size, media and visible presenter cues. Separately assert four-beat order, exact source strings, row and thumbnail counts and computed cumulative totals. Inspect the longest benefit and every cumulative board visually; geometry alone misses adjacent text collisions.

Keep proof claims tied to dated artifacts and permission. Keep owner questions and unresolved values in private notes, not the audience deck. A plan for the slide deck is not a rendered, tested deck.
