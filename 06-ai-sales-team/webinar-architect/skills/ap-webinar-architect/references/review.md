# Review Mode: Diagnosing a Webinar from Transcript + Results

The review answers three questions in order: (1) WHERE does the funnel break? (2) WHY - which frameworks are missing or malformed in the transcript? (3) WHAT is the highest-use fix for the next version? Resist fixing everything at once - find the biggest broken stage, fix that, measure, iterate.

## Step 0: Inputs

**Required:** the transcript (and slide deck if available).
**Each metric supports a diagnostic - collect what exists, work with what you get:**

| Metric | Diagnostic |
|---|---|
| Registrations + show-up rate | Pre-webinar diagnosis (out of script scope, but name it) |
| Attendance/drop-off curve | Locates the exact minute the script loses people |
| Conversion rate (attendees → buyers) | Overall verdict vs. benchmarks |
| Price + offer type | Which benchmark applies; whether price timing was routed correctly |
| Traffic temperature (cold/warm) | Adjusts every expectation |
| Replay + cart-close stats | Post-webinar machine diagnosis |
| Refund rate | Overpromise / guarantee-mismatch signal |
| Chat log / Q&A recording | Close-discipline audit |

## Step 1: Funnel Diagnosis: locate the broken stage

Benchmarks from the source systems' published campaign math and live results. Treat as reference points claimed by practitioners, not laws - always compare against the user's own previous versions first when history exists.

| Stage | Reference point | If well below → look at |
|---|---|---|
| Registration → show-up | ~25% (cold traffic); warm/email audiences run higher | Reminder emails, indoctrination sequence, calendar friction - NOT the script |
| Hold through minute 10 | Most psychological drop-off happens in the first 3 minutes | Hook (Section 1) |
| Hold to the pitch (~min 45-50) | Attendance should decline gently, not cliff | Content section engagement |
| Attendees → buyers | ~10% at $997 (the classic benchmark); 2-5% common for first webinars; top practitioners have hit 9% on a $3K offer; a bare stack-less pitch converts ~5-10% of what a full stack+close does. No calibrated sub-$1K benchmark exists in the source systems - but conversion should rise as price falls, so a sub-$1K offer converting below the 2-5% first-webinar band points squarely at Sections 4-8 | Transition through Q&A (Sections 4-8) |
| Replay contribution | Replays roughly DOUBLE total sales when a Fri-Sun replay + Sunday-midnight cart close exists | Post-webinar sequence |
| Refunds | Persistent high refunds | Overpromising in Gain/Offer, or wrong guarantee type |

A first-run webinar underperforming its final form is normal and expected: repeated live runs can reveal which sections need revision. The review's job is to make each rep count.

## Step 2: Transcript Audit: count the countables

Score each skeleton section 0-10. These markers are objective - count them in the transcript before judging quality:

**Global**
- [ ] Big Domino: can you state, from the transcript alone, the ONE belief the webinar argues? If you find two or more competing beliefs, that alone can explain a failed pitch.
- [ ] Micro-commitments/tie-downs through content: count them. Target 15-20 (one per 2-3 min). Under 5 = passive audience.
- [ ] Jargon test: sentences a smart 12-year-old couldn't parse; missing "it's kind of like..." bridges.

**Hook (0-3 min)** - What happens in the first 180 seconds? Bio or logistics opening = automatic fail. Count open loops created (target 3+, one per content pillar). Is there a deliberate opening device (Big Promise Cold Open / Callout + Disqualify / Proof-First / Story Cold Open / Cards On The Table / Pop Quiz / rapport frame)? **Format match:** a chat-dependent quiz on a recorded or automated webinar is a mismatch - flag it even if the rest of the hook scores well. Is there a quantified promise by minute 3 (cold opens land it inside the first 90 seconds)?

**Intro (3-15 min)** - Pain: second-person specificity (Pain Mirror) or generic "we all struggle"? Capped at ~5 min? · Gain: numbers and timeframes, or vague transformation? · Position: rapid proof stack (10-15 points) or credential-dumping? Stated motive? · Origin story: all 8 Story Arc beats? Conflict present? Ends on transformation or just the money number?

**Content (15-45 min)** - Which framework, and does it match the offer type (stories for B2C-info, teaching for B2B/SaaS)? Fatal check: **does it teach the HOW?** (If viewers could implement alone, content cannibalized the offer - the #1 cause of "great feedback, no sales.") Mechanisms 3-5 and under 15 min each? Objection-busting reps ("you're probably thinking...") - count them.

**Transition (45-48 min)** - Recap present (5-7 transformation-phrased bullets)? Tie-down cascade (~6 escalating yeses)? Two Choices (or permission question) - or an abrupt "so anyway, I have a product"? Apologetic selling language ("I hate this part but...") is a red flag on its own.

**Offer (48-55 min)** - One-sentence overview? ≤7 components? Value anchored BEFORE price (four-tier descent or marinade+If/All)? Price timing matched to price point (early <$5K, delayed $5K+)?

**Stack (55-75 min)** - Count Stack Slide recurrences: target 6+, once at the end = broken. Bonuses 3-7, each mapped to a named objection? Modality diversity (at least one S/A-tier)? Tools vs. more-training balance? Guarantee present, actively sold, after value? Scarcity: real, with a stated reason why?

**Close (75-90 min)** - Count the asks: target 26+; most failing webinars have 2-3. Count distinct closes deployed and check rotation (price → time → belief → identity, not one objection hammered). Testimonials followed by 4-6 trial closes each, or left bare? CTA URL continuously visible from price reveal?

**Q&A Close (90+ min)** - Does it exist at all? (Its absence forfeits the highest-converting segment.) Questions answered literally (customer service) or diagnosed and reframed (sales)? Urgency restated? Strong final CTA, or a fade-out?

## Step 3: Symptom → Diagnosis → Fix

| Symptom | Likely diagnosis | Fix (framework) |
|---|---|---|
| Big drop in first 10 min | Bio/logistics opening; no stakes; no loops; hook/format mismatch | Rebuild hook to match format: recorded → Big Promise Cold Open (or Proof-First); live with chat → Pop Quiz targeting top-5 objections (sections-open.md) |
| Steady bleed through content | Tutorial-mode teaching; mechanisms too deep; no engagement | Cut the "how"; add tie-downs every 2-3 min; 4-Step Demos (sections-open.md) |
| Cliff exactly at the pitch | Abrupt transition; "here comes the pitch" resistance | Install 3-Step Protocol: Recap → Tie-Downs → Two Choices (sections-close.md) |
| Held to the end, didn't buy | Offer/stack/close underpowered: single stack showing, unanchored price, few asks | Stack recurrence 6+, price anchoring, 26+ asks in rotating waves, add Q&A Close (sections-close.md + closes.md) |
| Great feedback, no sales | Content produced gratitude, not identity shift; taught the how; or multiple Big Dominoes | Strategic depth control; collapse to ONE belief; convert lessons into belief-breaking stories |
| Engaged chat, price pushback dominating | Value-to-price gap: weak stack, no cost-of-inaction framing | Rebuild stack with belief-mapped bonuses; deploy price-category closes (#21, #22, #24) |
| Sales but high refunds | Overpromise in Gain/Offer; wrong guarantee type | Micro-wins over macro-promises; consider conditional guarantee |
| Q&A killed momentum | Literal answers; URL off-screen; no deadline restating | Objection-diagnosis discipline; answer material questions before requesting payment; scarcity reminders every few minutes |
| Low show-up but strong conversion | Script is fine - pre-webinar machine is broken | Reminder cadence, indoctrination content, calendar hooks (out of script scope - say so) |
| Live converts, replay/automated doesn't | Live charisma masking missing structure; no spliced trial closes | Splice 5-15 sec trial-close clips at proof moments (+75% case); prepare 8-12 common-question responses |

## Step 4: The Review Report

Deliver in this exact shape:

```
# Webinar Review: {name} - {date}

**Verdict:** {one sentence: where it breaks and the single highest-use fix}

## Funnel Diagnosis
{stage table with user's numbers vs. reference points; the broken stage flagged}

## Section Scorecard
| Section | Score /10 | Evidence from transcript | Framework gap |

## Top Fixes (prioritized)
1. {Fix} - {framework citation} - {why this mechanism raises the metric}
   (3-5 items, ordered by expected impact, not by ease)

## What's Working: Do Not Touch
{list - protect these in the rewrite}

## Next Version Plan
{ONE major variable to change + supporting tweaks; what metric should move and by roughly how much}
```

## Iteration Discipline

- **One major variable per version.** Change the hook AND the offer AND the close, and the results teach you nothing. Attribution requires isolation.
- **Keep every version** (`v1/`, `v2/`...) - never overwrite; you WILL want to revert.
- **Log each version:** what changed, why (framework citation), what happened. The log is institutional memory that prevents repeating dead ends.
- **Benchmark against your own previous version first**, source benchmarks second.
- **Iterate live before automating** - automate only after conversion stabilizes, then inject spliced trial closes and pre-scripted Q&A closes into the recording.
