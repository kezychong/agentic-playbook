# Slide Architecture

A webinar slide is a **pacing tool, not a document**. One headline, one image, or 2-3 bullets max; each slide = 5-20 seconds of talking; the presenter narrates OVER the slides. Basis: the classic full-length template (~194 slides across 66 structural positions) and real high-converting decks (60-130 slides, close section = 25-35% of the deck).

**Total target: 150-220 slides.** Under ~120 slides = under-built. Every slide in a script carries a `[SLIDE VISUAL: ...]` tag describing exactly what's on screen.

## Section Budgets

| Section | Slides | ~Share | Notes |
|---------|--------|--------|-------|
| Hook | 8-12 | ~5% | Cold-open slides are fast: callout, promise, proof flash, roadmap. Pop Quiz (live only) = one slide per question |
| Intro (Pain+Gain+Position+Origin) | 20-30 | ~13% | Origin Story alone = 12-15 |
| Content (3 pillars/secrets) | 40-50 | ~24% | Each secret = 12-15 story slides + 5-10 objection-bust slides |
| Transition | 4-6 | ~3% | Recap re-shows content slides with overlay titles |
| Offer | 25-35 | ~16% | Each component = 4-6 slides + recurring Stack Slide |
| Stack (bonuses+guarantee+scarcity) | 20-30 | ~13% | Each bonus = 3-5 slides + recurring Stack Slide |
| Close waves | 20-30 | ~13% | Cycles + testimonial clusters + CTAs |
| Q&A Close | 15-25 | ~11% | 8-12 closes × 2 slides + CTA slides |
| **Total** | **150-220** | | Offer+Stack+Close+Q&A ≈ half the deck |

## Story Sequence: every 8-Step Story Arc = 12-15 slides

Never compress a story into 1-4 text-heavy slides; each beat gets its own slide while the presenter narrates:

1. Story setup headline ("[Name]'s Story") · 2. Character photo ("before" life) · 3. Desire - one bold line · 4. Old vehicle - icon/image of what they tried · 5. **Wall** - frustration photo + hard stat ("$47K spent, zero results") · 6. Epiphany moment - simple diagram/insight line · 7. Before/after belief bridge diagram · 8. New belief - full-screen bold sentence · 9. Plan/action · 10. Conflict - doubt quote or setback image · 11. **Achievement** - bold specific numbers · 12. Transformation - "after" photo or identity quote · 13. **Proof screenshot** - real evidence, highlight circled · 14. Paradigm-shift headline restating the belief · 15. Trial close ("Which detail of this story feels familiar?")

Pacing: setup slides fast (~5-8 sec), wall/epiphany slow (~15-20 sec, emotional weight), results/proof hold longest. Analytical/B2B audiences: tighten to 10-12 slides with more proof; emotional/B2C: full 15 with more photos.

## Offer Component Sequence: 4-6 slides each

1. Component intro (name + icon/product image) · 2. Origin/reason-why ("I built this because...") · 3. Benefits - 3 outcome bullets max ("You'll be able to..." / "You'll never have to... again") · 4. Proof (testimonial/screenshot/case data for THIS component) · 5. Value anchor ("Value: $X" large) · 6. **Running Stack Slide**.

## Bonus Sequence: 3-5 slides each

1. "BONUS #X" intro + benefit-driven name · 2. Benefits + future pacing ("Picture yourself opening this Friday...") · 3. Value anchor · 4. **Updated Stack Slide** · 5. Optional trial close or testimonial. Best/final bonus gets +1-2 buildup slides (extended origin or dramatic proof, exclusivity framing).

## The Stack Slide: the core persuasion mechanism

A cumulative list of every component and bonus revealed so far, each with its value, plus a growing running total (bold, red, underlined - identical layout every time so it's instantly recognized). **It appears after EVERY component and EVERY bonus - 6+ times total.** Test recurrence against your own audience and track the result. The audience watching the pile of value grow IS the mechanism - showing it once at the end is the #1 slide-design failure. Final full stack (maximum value number) lands immediately before the price reveal; the stack reappears once more with the guarantee added, and optionally at the final scarcity push.

## Guarantee: 2-3 slides

1. Guarantee headline ("YOUR 30-DAY PROMISE") · 2. Terms in plain human language, 3-4 lines, no legalese · 3. Stack Slide + "PLUS: your money-back guarantee" line.

## Close Cycles: 2-3 slides per cycle

1. Objection stated in the audience's exact words, big bold text on dark background ("I DON'T HAVE THE TIME") · 2. Reframe - the deployed close, with proof or comparison visual · 3. CTA - button graphic + URL. Insert testimonial clusters (3-5 slides) BETWEEN cycles; every cluster ends with a CTA slide.

## Q&A Close: 2 slides per close

1. "Question" slide (audience-voice quote format) · 2. "Answer" slide (reframe headline or proof image) + 60-120 sec spoken close. CTA slide every 3-4 closes.

## Visual Patterns (from real high-converting decks)

- **Proof density:** ~1 proof slide (screenshot, number, photo) per 2-3 assertion slides. Never 3+ consecutive slides without a concrete visual.
- **Background alternation:** light backgrounds for evidence (screenshots, data), dark for interpretation (bold beliefs, paradigm statements) - the rhythm subconsciously separates proof from claim.
- **Progressive highlight:** between content pillars, re-show the "3 Secrets" overview with completed ones checked - a "you are here" device that appears 3-4 times.
- **Real screenshots beat designed graphics** for all proof - messages, dashboards, bank apps, with a red circle/arrow on the key number.
- **CTA frequency:** URL on-screen continuously from price reveal onward; never more than 2-3 slides from a CTA in the close; final CTA slide = URL as the largest text in the entire deck.

## Anti-Patterns: never do these

- A story on fewer than 10 slides (too compressed - break it down)
- A component or bonus crammed onto one slide
- The Stack Slide shown only once
- Any slide without a `[SLIDE VISUAL:]` tag
- Slides written as reading documents (walls of text kill pacing)
