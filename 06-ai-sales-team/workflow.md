# Sales workflow: evidence to draft

Agentic Playbook — by Kezy

Begin with audience, offer, evidence and the requested next action. Do not invent demand,
testimonials, revenue claims, urgency, discounts or guarantees.

- Before a conversation, [Pre-meeting Brief](pre-meeting-brief/skills/ap-pre-meeting-brief/SKILL.md) checks only
  allowed accounts and notes. It separates dated facts, open promises, inferences and unread sources.
- After a conversation, [Call to Outcome](call-to-outcome/skills/ap-call-to-outcome/SKILL.md) produces the
  requested proposal, follow-up or action plan. Preserve source quotations and keep missing owners,
  prices and deadlines awaiting confirmation. Transcription and account setup are optional approved steps.
- To package a cohort offer, [Challenge Offer Creation](challenge-offer-creation/skills/ap-challenge-offer-creation/SKILL.md)
  preserves five moves: intake; offer design; funnel parameters; actual delivery assets; back-end
  continuity. An offer specification is not a published checkout or launch campaign.
- For an appointment funnel, [VSL Book a Call](vsl-book-a-call/skills/ap-vsl-book-a-call/SKILL.md) uses the complete
  doctrine, intake templates and optional Python gate. Read the gate before running it; syntax checks
  on this package do not prove it judges sales quality. The call promise must match delivery capacity.
- For a teaching-led sales event, [Webinar Architect](webinar-architect/skills/ap-webinar-architect/SKILL.md) carries
  intake through opening, content, transition, close, slides and review. Its reference files preserve
  the detailed sequence and progressive stack. Claims and scarcity still require truthful support.

Save the chosen draft alongside its evidence and unresolved questions. Review terms and proof
before any external send, landing-page publication or calendar/CRM change. This package does none
of those actions. Use the blank brief and fictional rehearsal before introducing permitted client data.

Update by comparing versions and preserving your local settings outside the distributed template.
Remove the selected host skill folder only after approval; removing it does not retract sent material
or revoke connected account access.
