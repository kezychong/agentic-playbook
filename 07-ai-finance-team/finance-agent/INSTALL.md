# Your Finance Agent - Install

## Who this is for
- A business owner who is smart at business and not technical.
- You already have your agent running and answering you in chat.
- You work through the Claude app or the Codex app. Your agent does all setup itself.

## What is in this folder
- finance-agent-onboarding-pack - the part YOU read. Six short files that walk you through setup.
- finance-agent-distribution - the part your AGENT reads. Its identity, rules, and skills.

## Stage before installation
Point your agent at this folder and say:

"Read INSTALL.md, then stage my Finance Agent templates after the onboarding interview. Start at finance-agent-onboarding-pack/START-HERE.md. Do not install or connect anything."

Your agent takes it from there. You answer its questions in plain words.

## For the agent doing the install
Read finance-agent-onboarding-pack/START-HERE.md and walk the owner through it in order.
Stage a separate copy of finance-agent-distribution for review, filling every
{{PLACEHOLDER}} from the owner's questionnaire answers. Keep everything read-only before and after the readiness checklist. Profile installation needs separate approval and current host-schema verification. Never move money. Never send an email yourself.
