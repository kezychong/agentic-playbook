# Start Here - Your Finance Agent Onboarding

Welcome. This pack takes you from zero to a finance agent that knows your books, reads your
real numbers, and never touches your money.

Read this file first. Setup time depends on inputs and host support; no duration is guaranteed.

## Who this is for
- A business owner who is smart at business and not technical. You do not need to be.
- You already have your agent running and answering you in chat.
- You work through the Claude app or the Codex app on your laptop.
- Your agent stages a draft setup for review. No login or live profile change is needed for the fictional rehearsal.

## What this is
A guided setup pack. By the end you will have:
- Your agent reading your real books, or your real statements if you keep no software.
- A monthly close it runs with you, step by step.
- A weekly money report: cash in, cash out, what is overdue.
- A monthly profit and cashflow statement.
- A signed money-safety agreement so the agent never moves a cent.

## What this is not
- Not your accountant. It prepares and proves numbers. A qualified human still signs off.
- Not a payment tool. It never moves money, never pays anyone, never files anything with a
  government office.
- Not an email sender. It drafts invoice emails and overdue chasers. You press send.
- Host, model and connector charges depend on your existing accounts; verify costs before use.

## What you need before you start
1. Your agent already running and answering you in chat.
2. The Claude app or the Codex app on the laptop you work on.
3. Owner or admin access to your accounting software, if you use one.
4. If you use no software: your bank statements and your invoice list, in any form you can
   export or photograph.
5. Two facts about your business: the date your financial year ends, and how long clients
   normally have to pay you.

If you do not know 5, look it up now. It takes two minutes and everything downstream uses it.

## No software is a real route
If you keep no accounting software, you are not stuck and you are not second class. Manual mode
is fully supported. Once a month you hand your agent your statements and your invoice list, and
it runs the exact same close, the exact same reports, the exact same checks.

A connection does one thing: it removes the copying. That is the whole difference.

## One concept to hold onto
An invoice marked PAID does not prove money arrived.

Think of a bill stamped SETTLED. That stamp does not tell you whether the customer paid you or
whether you tore the bill up. Accounting software stamps both the same way. A credit note, which
is the formal way of cancelling part or all of a bill, leaves an invoice looking exactly like a
paid one: amount due zero, status paid.

So your agent is built to never say a bare "paid". It says one of two things:
- "Paid, cash received on 14 March."
- "Credit noted, no cash received."

Before it claims either, it checks the invoice record for credit notes and looks for a matching
deposit in the bank. This one habit is the difference between a report you can trust and a
report that quietly lies to you about your own cash.

## Read-only first, always
Your agent starts able to look and not touch. It can read invoices, payments, cash position and
profit. It cannot create or change anything.

Ledger writes remain disabled after the checklist. Readiness means the owner has verified the reporting process, not granted authority to change books.
